A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Still curious? We like that about you.

Is AURA going to write everything for me?

Nope. Think of AURA as a thoughtful writing partner. It helps you find a starting point, tune a sentence, or try a new tone. The final words are always yours.

What can I write with AURA?

Emails, notes, posts, speeches, tricky messages, and all the little bits of writing that make up a day. If it needs words, AURA can help you get there.

Can I change my plan later?

Absolutely. Start on the free plan and move to AURA plus whenever you need more room. You can change or cancel your plan at any time.

Do my words still sound like me?

That’s the whole idea. AURA offers a first draft, not a final say. Take what works, change what doesn’t, and keep the words that feel like you.
